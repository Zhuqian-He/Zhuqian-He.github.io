### 📊 Econometric Notes & Identification

#### 2. Handling Fixed Effects in Predictions Under High-Dimensional Fixed Effects (HDFE) Models
* **Original Title:** HDFE模型在对因变量进行预测时，如何处理固定效应？
* **Link:** [Read Original Article (Chinese)](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/NkvL8Yyy2PewtI8nDgNeHQ)
* **Themes:** High-Dimensional Fixed Effects, HDFE Prediction, reghdfe, fixest, Average Partial Effects
* **Core Intuition:** 
  A deep dive into the econometric logic of predictions in HDFE models. Grounded in Wooldridge's Average Partial Effects (APE) framework, I unpack why Stata's `reghdfe` and R's `fixest` predict net of fixed effects to isolate covariate-driven average conditional means.
